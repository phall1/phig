# Code of conduct

Phig uses the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.
Examples of acceptable behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Harassment,
sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, doxxing, and publishing
private information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, private where
possible, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
address on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
kept confidential to the extent practical. Do not use a public issue for a
conduct report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in
public. Attribution and enforcement guidance follow Contributor Covenant 2.1.
